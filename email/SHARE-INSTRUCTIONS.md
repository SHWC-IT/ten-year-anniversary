# Inviting people yourself

Thank you for helping invite people to our 10th Anniversary. Here are three ways to do it,
easiest first. Any of them is fine, pick whichever suits you.

---

## The easiest way: forward the invitation

You will receive a copy of the invitation by email. It says **"Hello Friend"** rather than a name,
so it reads correctly for whoever you send it to.

1. Open the email
2. Press **Forward**
3. Type your friend's email address
4. Add a line of your own at the top, something like *"Hi Kwame, I would love you to join us"*
5. Send

That is it. The invitation arrives looking exactly as it should.

**A personal line at the top matters more than you would think.** People come because someone they
know asked them, not because they received a nice-looking email.

## If you would rather write a fresh email

Use this if you want the full designed invitation inside an email you compose yourself. Do this on
a computer rather than a phone.

1. Open this page in your browser:
   ```
   ten.theshepherdshouse.church/email/invite
   ```
2. Click anywhere on the page, then press **Ctrl + A** (on a Mac, **Cmd + A**). Everything
   highlights.
3. Press **Ctrl + C** (Mac: **Cmd + C**) to copy.
4. Start a new email in Gmail or Outlook.
5. Click in the empty message area and press **Ctrl + V** (Mac: **Cmd + V**).
6. The whole invitation appears. Add a greeting at the top if you like, then send.

### One box to check first

If your email arrives as plain text with no colours, formatting was switched off. Check this before
you paste:

- **Gmail**: in the compose window, click the three dots at the bottom right. If **Plain text mode**
  has a tick next to it, click it to turn it off.
- **Outlook**: on the **Format Text** tab, make sure **HTML** is selected rather than Plain Text.

### What it will look like

Very close to the original, with two small differences you can safely ignore:

- The fancy script lettering becomes an italic serif, because those fonts only exist on our website.
- On a phone it will not resize to fit the screen the way the original does. This is why forwarding
  is the better option if your friends read email on their phones.

Everything else carries over: the colours, the gold Register button, the schedule, and all the
links.
